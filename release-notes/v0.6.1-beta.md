## Nebsty Planner 0.6.1 beta

**⬇︎ Download:** `Nebsty-Planner-0.6.1-beta.dmg` under Assets below
**Requires:** macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon / Intel
**Time limit:** this beta works for 30 days from its build date. Your data stays where it is.

If you already have 0.5.0, just drag the new app into Applications and replace the old one — your data folder and settings are kept.

### New
- **Google Tasks & Google Calendar sync** — connect your Google account in ⚙ Settings › External sync. Works alongside Apple Reminders & Calendar; task lists and calendars map to `#tags` the same way.
- **Dated to-dos in memos & projects** — write `- [ ] Install the wiki #work 📅 2026-10-12` in any memo or project document and it shows up on that day in the daily, weekly and monthly views, without copying it into the daily page.
  - Click it to jump to the memo; check it off and the memo line gets `[x]` and `✅ completion date` (the Obsidian Tasks format).
  - It syncs to Apple Reminders and Google Tasks with that due date. Deleting it in Reminders keeps the to-do in your memo and only removes the date.
- **Type dates quickly** — on a to-do line, `@10-12`, `@2026-10-12`, `@today` or `@tomorrow` turns into `📅 date` as soon as you type a space. A sample memo explains it.
- **Automatic update check** — the app now tells you when a new beta is out.

### Changed
- The **system font** is now the default. To use another font (for example NanumSquareRound or Pretendard), choose **Custom** and type the name of a font installed on your computer. No fonts are bundled any more.
- The ⚙ Settings window fits smaller screens, and the ⟳ sync button spins evenly.

### Known issues
- The beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch needs **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**.
- Google sign-in shows a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen during the beta — click **Advanced › Go to Nebsty Planner**.
- Recurring events are not imported by calendar sync (they do show from subscribed calendars).

### 한국어 요약
- **Google Tasks · Google 캘린더 동기화** — ⚙ 설정 › 외부 동기화에서 Google 계정 연결.
- **메모 · 프로젝트의 📅 할 일** — `- [ ] 할 일 #태그 📅 2026-10-12` 처럼 쓰면 그날의 일간 · 주간 · 월간에 보이고, 누르면 그 메모로, 체크하면 메모에 `✅`. 미리 알림 · Google Tasks 와도 그 마감일로 동기화.
- **날짜 빨리 넣기** — 할 일 줄에 `@10-12` · `@오늘` · `@내일` 을 쓰고 칸을 띄우면 `📅 날짜` 로. 샘플 메모 포함.
- **업데이트 알림** — 새 베타가 나오면 앱이 알려 줍니다.
- 글꼴은 **시스템 글꼴**이 기본, 다른 글꼴은 **직접 입력**으로 (나눔스퀘어라운드 · Pretendard 등 설치된 글꼴).
- 설정 창이 작은 화면에서도 넘치지 않고, ⟳ 동기화 버튼이 고르게 돕니다.
