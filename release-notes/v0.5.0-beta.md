## Nebsty Planner 0.5.0 beta

The first beta. Please try it and leave feedback in [Issues](https://github.com/yang83/nebsty-planner/issues).

**⬇︎ Download:** `Nebsty-Planner-0.5.0-beta.dmg` under Assets below
**Requires:** macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon / Intel
**Time limit:** this beta works for 30 days from its build date. When it ends, your data stays where it is.

### Install
1. Open the DMG and drag Nebsty Planner into Applications.
2. If macOS warns on first launch, go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway** (needed once, because the beta isn't notarized yet).

### What's inside
- **Top-down planner** — mission & long-term goals → year → month → week → day, in an open-notebook layout (⌘1–8)
- **Schedule** — drag to create, move and resize events; drag a task onto the grid to time-block it
- **Habits & recurring events**, weekly habit check-offs
- **Memos & projects** — Markdown documents, WBS schedule with a Gantt chart, paste from Excel
- **Apple Reminders & Calendar sync** — today's due to-dos and events. Reminder lists and calendars ↔ `#tags`; untagged items go to the inbox / default calendar
- **Subscribed calendars (ICS)** — holidays, school or team calendars, read-only
- **Images** — drop or paste into memos, daily notes and project documents; drag a corner to resize
- **Backup & year-end cleanup**, change the data folder (iCloud, Nextcloud)
- **Font picker**, light / dark mode, English / 한국어
- Update notifications

### Known issues
- The beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch needs "Open Anyway".
- When you switch to a new beta, macOS may ask again for Reminders and Calendar access.
- Recurring events are not imported by Apple Calendar sync (they do show from subscribed calendars).
